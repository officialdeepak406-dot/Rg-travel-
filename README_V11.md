# RG Tempo Traveller — V11

V11 keeps the existing premium white design and adds a larger South India travel library.

## New Signature Trips
- Karnataka: Coorg, Chikmagalur, Mysuru, Hampi
- Kerala: Munnar, Alleppey, Wayanad, Thekkady

## New City Destination Pages
Each new city card opens a dedicated page.

## Whole-card navigation
- Tempo fleet cards: click anywhere on the card to open the vehicle page.
- Signature trip cards: click anywhere on the card to open the trip planner.
- City destination cards: click anywhere on the card to open the destination page.

## Destination images
New destination image slots are in `images/destinations/`. They are clearly marked SVG placeholders so you can replace them with real photographs without changing HTML. Use the same filenames:
`coorg-01.svg` … `coorg-04.svg`, etc.

## Content basis
Route themes and attraction selections were planned from current Karnataka Tourism and Kerala Tourism destination information. Exact timings, hotel availability, entry rules and road conditions should be confirmed before booking.
